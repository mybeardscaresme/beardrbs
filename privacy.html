BeardRBS Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Overview

BeardRBS is a personal, noncommercial health analytics project intended for use by its owner.

Information Accessed

With authorization, BeardRBS may retrieve sleep, activity, readiness, heart rate, blood oxygen, stress, and cardiovascular health information through the Oura API.

Data Storage and Use

Health information retrieved by BeardRBS is intended to be stored and analyzed locally on the owner's personal computer. This public website does not collect or store Oura health records.

Third-Party Services

Oura provides the health data through its API. GitHub Pages hosts this informational website. Health information will not be sold or shared with advertisers.

Data Control

The account owner can revoke application access through their Oura account and delete locally stored data.

Contact

Questions about this application can be directed to the contact email registered with the Oura developer application.
